01  The Bottom Line
PJM is moving on two fronts at once: a large-load registry and a backstop procurement auction scheduled for October, while the capacity market just hit its price cap for the third straight time at $16.4 billion. The NextEra-Dominion deal, Amazon's push to fund its own transmission, and the QTS-Compass collapse at Prince William all point to the same conclusion: the grid constraint is real, the queue is broken, and the capital is restructuring around both facts. Developers who are not already positioned at viable POIs in PJM are not catching up by waiting.

Amazon's push to fund transmission via CIAC in Dominion's Rider T1 proceeding is a consequential position regardless of whether the Virginia SCC acts on it in this case. 05PolicyPJM to file two FERC proposals creating a large load registry and Reliability Backstop Procurement auction scheduled for October 2026.The Read▾
PJM Interconnection · 2026-07-29
PJM's board is directing two FERC filings: a large-load registry and a Reliability Backstop Procurement auction targeted for October 2026. The board rejected the stakeholder-approved subscription model in favor of including Interim Resource Adequacy Service, which is a more aggressive posture toward forcing large loads to internalize their capacity costs. October is an aggressive timeline for a new auction structure that still needs FERC approval. The registry alone is significant because it creates a formal data obligation for large loads that currently have no standardized reporting requirement. Both filings are worth tracking closely.

SPP  · 1 signal
01PolicyNebraska Gov. Pillen signs executive order blocking new data center tax incentives under ImagiNE Act, citing power and water resource concerns.The Read▾
State of Nebraska · 2026-07-29
Nebraska's executive order blocking new data center tax incentives under the ImagiNE Act is the clearest state-level signal yet that the subsidy era for large digital infrastructure is over in power-constrained markets. The order does not ban data centers; it removes the tax preference and creates a joint agency review of power and water impacts. Developers who built Nebraska site strategies around the incentive stack need to reprice those projects. More broadly, the political calculus in SPP territory is shifting: power availability and grid impact are now the primary screening criteria, not the incentive schedule.

04PolicyIESO imposing new reliability standards including voltage ride-through on large data centers via connection assessments ahead of formal market rule…The Read▾
IESO · 2026-07-29
IESO is applying voltage ride-through standards to large data center connection assessments before the formal market rule changes are complete. This is how reliability requirements actually get embedded in practice: informally first, then codified. Ontario developers who assumed they could meet legacy connection standards on large computational loads are going to find those assumptions challenged in the assessment phase. The U.S. parallel is ERCOT's large-load technical requirements, which tightened faster than developers expected. Get ahead of the technical requirements before the formal rules lock them in.
