01  The Bottom Line
The capital is committed and the load is real: hyperscalers, AI developers, and utilities across every major ISO are now publishing GW-scale numbers with signed agreements and cash deposits behind them. The constraint has shifted entirely to interconnection timelines and reliability standards, as the Northern Virginia ride-through event and PJM's capacity auction hitting the price cap for the third straight cycle make clear. Developers who are not already holding a queue position at the right POI are paying a premium for someone else's foresight.

03Policy3GW of Virginia data center load tripped offline during a grid fault, prompting NERC to push mandatory ride-through standards for data centers.The Read▾
NERC · 2026-07-28
Three gigawatts of data center load dropping off in Northern Virginia during a single transmission fault is the reliability event that turns NERC's ride-through standard from a proposal into a mandate. The grid stabilized, but the mechanism that saved it was voltage recovery speed, not facility design. Every existing data center UPS and control system that prioritizes IT equipment protection over grid stability is now a compliance liability, and every new facility design that does not incorporate ride-through capability from day one will face retrofit costs. The demand for battery storage as a ride-through buffer, not just as backup power, just got a very public endorsement from the grid event itself.

Pepco · 2026-07-27
Utility ROE compression is a real transmission financing risk that tends to get underweighted in long-horizon power procurement models. That is the dynamic worth tracking here, not the Pepco rate case itself.
