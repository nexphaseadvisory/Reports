Held for review
Two signals define the day: Abbott's freeze on ERCOT's batch process injects maximum uncertainty into the largest large-load interconnection pipeline in the country, and PJM's reliability backstop framework is advancing toward FERC while capacity prices stay capped at records. Abbott directing ERCOT and the PUC to conduct a comprehensive verification and audit of all data centers advancing through the batch process is the most disruptive near-term event in U.S. power market site strategy.
