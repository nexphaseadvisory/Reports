01  The Bottom Line
PJM is moving to make large loads pay for their own supply, ERCOT is codifying curtailment rules for co-location, and hyperscaler capex is still accelerating hard. The market is bifurcating: developers who arrive at a site with contracted power and a credible interconnection position will transact, and everyone else will wait in a queue that keeps getting longer while regulators assign them the bill for new capacity.

Mon Power socializing the cost of $2.7 billion in new West Virginia generation onto existing ratepayers, mainly to serve a single data center, is the most aggressive version of a cost-allocation fight playing out across PJM right now. FirstEnergy disclosed contracted data center load is up 50% in a single quarter to 6.4 GW, with another 1.5 GW expected to close within weeks. Panamint Capital · 2026-07-30
Panamint's play at Bremond is textbook brownfield repricing: acquire a retiring coal site, retain the existing interconnection and site infrastructure, and stack solar on top. The 800 MW of adjacent gas capacity available for data center offtake is the commercial hook. We have seen this structure attempted before in MISO and PJM, where the legacy interconnection right is the real asset being monetized and the new generation is the wrapper. In ERCOT, that legacy position is genuinely valuable given queue depth. Whether Panamint can actually execute offtake agreements against that gas capacity depends on whether the Twin Oaks plant's interconnection rights survive the transition cleanly, and that detail is worth confirming before any procurement conversation goes far.

U.S. Department of Energy / Utility Industry · 2026-07-29
Rolling back the 2024 transformer efficiency rule would slow the shift toward amorphous steel and keep GOES as the dominant input, which matters because transformer lead times are already a constraint on new interconnections.
