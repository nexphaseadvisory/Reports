Bottom Line
The AI data center power demand story continues to accelerate, with multi-gigawatt campus announcements, coal-to-compute conversions, and behind-the-meter generation strategies dominating developer activity across PJM, MISO, and emerging Southeast markets. Power procurement sophistication across the developer landscape remains uneven, with a significant cohort of growth-stage and newly capitalized operators still building internal energy functions from scratch. Interconnection queue strategy, PPA structuring, and generation-side origination are increasingly the critical path variables separating projects that break ground from those that stall. Early-stage and stealth-mode compute providers are also entering the market in Europe, signaling that the power procurement challenge is becoming a global infrastructure constraint, not solely a domestic one. The gap between capital availability and power-origination expertise is widening, sustaining strong market conditions for specialized advisory capacity.

Read  An 8GW buildout at a single campus represents one of the largest discrete power procurement mandates in the domestic data center market, and the filing's disclosure of nascent internal energy capability signals that external origination and interconnection advisory support remains a live need. The West Virginia location places this project within PJM territory, where interconnection queue dynamics and generation-side structuring are complex and high-stakes.
Strategic Thought  Campuses of this scale require layered power strategies — utility service agreements, behind-the-meter generation, long-dated PPAs, and potentially FERC-jurisdictional transmission arrangements — that exceed the capacity of a newly formed internal energy team. The PJM interconnection queue in West Virginia is among the most congested and procedurally demanding in the country, creating durable demand for advisors with queue management and cost allocation expertise. IPO-stage disclosure requirements also create pressure to demonstrate power supply certainty to public market investors, which further elevates the urgency and visibility of origination outcomes.
