Bottom Line
Offshore wind's regulatory environment has deteriorated sharply, with multistate litigation against the Trump administration and named developers creating acute portfolio-level uncertainty for lease holders. Simultaneously, AI-driven load growth continues to outpace utility planning cycles, widening the gap between data-center power demand and available interconnection capacity. Next-generation geothermal is crossing from pilot to commercial deployment, introducing a new class of IPP that will require interconnection and PPA structuring support in non-traditional geographies. Commercial building flexibility assets are drawing increased attention as a demand-response resource, though the aggregator market remains fragmented and unsophisticated. Across all four signals, the common thread is a market in structural transition—where advisory capability bridging regulatory, interconnection, and origination complexity carries measurable premium value.
