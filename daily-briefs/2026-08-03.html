Bottom Line
Hyperscaler AI capital deployment continues to accelerate, with Meta's revised $130–145B capex guidance reinforcing sustained power procurement pressure across U.S. grid markets. Simultaneously, state and county-level policy resistance is tightening the available development envelope, with Virginia debating a moratorium on new data center approvals and Florida's Treasure Coast joining a growing list of jurisdictions prohibiting new builds. Developers with executed interconnection rights and offtake agreements are increasingly differentiated in a market where site control alone is insufficient. The near-term advisory opportunity is concentrated in transactions already in motion, where timing certainty and transaction-close proximity define urgency.
