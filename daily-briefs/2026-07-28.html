Bottom Line
The U.S. power infrastructure market is experiencing simultaneous demand pressure across PJM, ERCOT, and emerging Mid-Atlantic corridors, with AI-driven data center campuses anchoring multi-gigawatt development pipelines that require sophisticated interconnection and origination strategy. The pending NextEra-Dominion merger is the single most consequential structural event in the near-term interconnection landscape, with the potential to reshape queue prioritization and PPA economics across Virginia, the Carolinas, and the broader PJM Southeast footprint through late 2027. Meanwhile, new AI campus groundbreakings in ERCOT and self-developed gigawatt-scale builds in PPA territories signal that power origination complexity is accelerating faster than most buyers' in-house capabilities. Independent advisory capacity positioned at the intersection of interconnection queues, generation asset transactions, and offtake structuring is in increasing demand across all major U.S. grid regions.
