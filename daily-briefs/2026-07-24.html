Bottom Line
The data center power gap is widening faster than project timelines can absorb: a 2GW Kentucky proposal from a six-week-old company has no executed PPAs and is still negotiating baseline supply with AEP, while Brookfield's $7B acquisition of Aypa Power consolidates 26.5GW of BESS capacity into a single platform with direct relevance to large-load interconnection structuring. Simultaneously, Sabey's first structural steel at SDC Umatilla marks the transition from entitlement to vertical construction on a 120MW-plus, $520M campus, compressing the window for upstream power advisory engagement. The Centuri-J.J. White combination signals that construction-side players are positioning for hyperscaler contract flow, reinforcing that the DC supply chain is scaling at every layer except power origination and interconnection strategy. Across all active signals, the consistent pattern is capital commitment preceding power certainty — a structural advisory gap that defines the current market center of gravity.

04  Market Signals · context, ranked by relevance
1
Brookfield Asset Management, Aypa Power, Blackstone
Brookfield Asset Management announced the acquisition of Aypa Power from Blackstone for approximately $7 billion, adding 6.5GW of operating and contracted battery energy storage and a 20GW-plus development pipeline to create a combined 26.5GW BESS platform across North America.
Airtable  Open record
Read  The transaction concentrates a significant share of utility-scale BESS capacity and pipeline under a single sophisticated infrastructure owner, raising the competitive bar for storage-firmed PPA structuring. Developers and large loads seeking storage-backed offtake will increasingly negotiate with fewer, larger counterparties.
Strategic Thought  For the interconnection advisory and power-origination market, Brookfield's consolidated platform creates a more liquid but also more concentrated counterparty environment for hybrid-power transactions where BESS co-location is used to firm renewable generation for large-load PPAs. Advisors with existing relationships across storage developers, transmission operators, and hyperscaler load desks will carry greater structural advantage as fewer independent BESS platforms remain available to anchor creative interconnection solutions. The deal also signals that capital is pricing storage-firmed capacity as a premium asset, which has direct implications for how power-origination terms are structured in future DC offtake agreements.
2
Rubix Data Centers  KENTUCKY
Rubix Data Centers, a subsidiary of Submer Group formed in June 2026, has proposed a up to 2GW, $12 billion data center campus at the former AK Steel site in Russell, Kentucky, with no executed power purchase agreements and ongoing negotiations with AEP for baseline supply.
Airtable  Open record
Read  A newly formed entity proposing gigawatt-scale load with no confirmed power solution and an acknowledged reliance on outside consultants for its planning application represents a textbook early-stage power advisory opening. The public acknowledgment that power is unsettled — at the same moment a planning application is being filed — creates an unusually transparent signal of need.
Strategic Thought  The Kentucky MISO/PJM boundary zone adds interconnection complexity that a nascent developer without in-house power expertise is poorly positioned to navigate, particularly for a load profile that would rank among the largest single interconnection requests in the region. The gap between proposed MW and secured offtake at this scale creates demand for advisors who can sequence interconnection queue strategy, negotiate with AEP on transmission service, and structure PPAs that satisfy both lender requirements and grid operator intake criteria. As more subsidiary-model developers enter the market with strong technology branding but thin power infrastructure experience, this pattern of MW ambition outpacing power certainty is likely to recur across multiple jurisdictions.
3
Centuri, J.J. White
Centuri announced the acquisition of J.J. White to expand its capabilities in data center and industrial construction markets.
Airtable  Open record
Read  The combination positions Centuri to compete for larger hyperscaler construction contracts by broadening its industrial services footprint, signaling continued consolidation on the construction side of the data center supply chain. Construction-side scale does not resolve upstream power and interconnection gaps that hyperscaler clients will still require resolved before shovel deployment.
Strategic Thought  As EPC and construction platforms scale to serve hyperscaler pipelines, the coordination surface between physical construction timelines and power delivery milestones grows more complex, creating demand for interconnection advisory that can synchronize with — rather than lag — construction schedules. Construction firms winning large DC contracts increasingly need credible power-sourcing partners to maintain client confidence through the full project lifecycle, particularly where interconnection studies and queue positions operate on timelines independent of permitting and civil construction. This dynamic elevates the strategic value of power-origination advisors who can engage at the pre-construction phase rather than being brought in reactively.
4
DG Matrix, Skeleton Technologies
DG Matrix and Skeleton Technologies announced a partnership to integrate supercapacitor-based energy storage with solid-state transformers for 800V DC power architecture in AI data center applications.
Airtable  Open record
Read  The emergence of 800V DC bus architectures with solid-state transformer integration represents a meaningful shift in how power is conditioned and distributed inside next-generation AI facilities, with implications for how generation assets interconnect at the facility boundary. While the technology is early-stage, its adoption trajectory will influence the technical specifications that power-origination agreements must eventually address.
Strategic Thought  For the interconnection advisory market, the proliferation of novel DC power architectures increases the technical heterogeneity between generation-side interconnection standards and facility-side intake requirements, a gap that will require advisors to maintain current working knowledge of load-side power specifications when structuring transmission service and offtake agreements. As AI-optimized facilities begin specifying non-standard voltage and conditioning requirements, the traditional boundary between generation advisory and facility power engineering will compress, rewarding advisors who can fluently translate between grid operator interconnection requirements and data center electrical architecture. This signal is currently low-actionability but merits monitoring as 800V DC deployments scale toward utility-relevant load sizes.
