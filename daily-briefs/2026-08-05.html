Bottom Line
The AI data center buildout is colliding simultaneously with regulatory enforcement, permitting opposition, and grid-access uncertainty across multiple U.S. markets, elevating the strategic value of interconnection-compliance and power-origination expertise. Texas, the largest deregulated market in North America, is now subject to a formal state-ordered audit of a 474GW large-load queue that is 90% data center, introducing material project-termination risk for developers without defensible interconnection structures. Parallel opposition in Oregon signals that land-control alone is insufficient without a credible power and regulatory strategy. Against this backdrop, newly capitalized platforms like Volta are racing to secure gigawatt-scale power positions before the compliance window tightens further. Established developers with active capital deployment — including Yondr Group and Origis Energy — represent the highest-conviction near-term engagement targets.
