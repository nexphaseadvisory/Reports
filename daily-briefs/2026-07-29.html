Bottom Line
The infrastructure capital market is accelerating on two parallel tracks: renewable energy platform consolidation is creating multi-gigawatt entities with complex origination and commodity management needs, while AI-driven data center demand is generating unprecedented power procurement and interconnection structuring pressure across ERCOT, PJM, and beyond. The MN8-Greenbacker combination exemplifies the consolidation dynamic, assembling a 6.2 GW portfolio that will require integrated commercial strategy across 33 states. Simultaneously, hyperscaler and crypto-adjacent developers are committing to nine- and ten-figure infrastructure programs that privately fund substation upgrades and stage multi-hundred-MW interconnection queues — a structural shift in how large loads approach grid access. Regulatory friction, illustrated by Montgomery County's moratorium, is forcing developers to seek more sophisticated policy and siting navigation alongside traditional power advisory. The combined effect is a market where origination expertise, interconnection structuring, and commodity strategy are simultaneously at a premium across both the generation and load sides of the market.
