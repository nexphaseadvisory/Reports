Bottom Line
Large-scale renewable and storage development continues to accelerate across multiple U.S. markets, with county-level and IRP-driven approvals signaling a maturing pipeline of near-term procurement and interconnection activity. Corporate clean energy buyers, particularly hyperscale data center operators, are under mounting pressure to reconcile reliability requirements with sustainability commitments, creating structural demand for firming and dispatchable clean power solutions. Utility IRP filings in Missouri, Michigan, and Colorado point to an imminent wave of BESS RFPs, compressing the window for advisors and developers to position ahead of formal solicitation. The 48E investment tax credit is accelerating behind-the-meter storage deployment, though many facility operators lack the grid-services expertise to fully monetize those assets. Across all these dynamics, interconnection strategy and offtake structuring remain the highest-leverage advisory intervention points in the market.
