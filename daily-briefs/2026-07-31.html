Bottom Line
The AI data center power market continues to accelerate across multiple vectors simultaneously — nuclear SMR partnerships, gas-fired BTM development, and land-assembly plays are all advancing in parallel, signaling that developers are pursuing every available path to secured, reliable load. Permitting and land-access risk is emerging as a distinct bottleneck, as illustrated by the Oregon Governor's termination of the Verrus land sale on a $5B project, forcing developers to rethink site selection and interconnection strategy mid-cycle. SMR-to-data-center partnerships are moving from conceptual to contractual, with the Crusoe-Aalo Atomics announcement marking the first formal deployment commitment at a national laboratory site, raising the bar for interconnection and licensing complexity. The 2 GW Beetaloo-Halliburton gas-to-power announcement in Australia underscores that data center power origination is now a global market, and that early-stage developers are openly recruiting specialist partners to structure generation, PPAs, and offtake. Across all signals, the common thread is that power origination and commercial structuring expertise — not capital — is the scarce resource at the table.

As fleet deployments scale, developers will require structured commercial frameworks for capacity allocation, curtailment protocols, and offtake across heterogeneous regulatory jurisdictions. The Crusoe-Aalo model, if replicated, will set market precedent for how SMR output is priced, hedged, and contracted into AI load — creating an early-mover advantage for advisors who build that structuring playbook now.

Airtable  Open record
Read  A gubernatorial termination of a land sale at this project scale is an acute commercial inflection point — Verrus must now simultaneously re-site, re-permit, and potentially re-engineer its interconnection and power-supply strategy under public scrutiny and time pressure. Re-siting a project of this scale requires parallel evaluation of interconnection queue position, available transmission capacity, and PPA counterparty availability in alternate geographies — a workflow that demands integrated power-origination expertise rather than siloed legal or real estate counsel. As state governments increase scrutiny of large data center land acquisitions, the market for advisors who can rapidly remodel power access and commercial structures around a forced site change will expand materially.

MISO's evolving large-load interconnection queue policies — including recent reforms to how new load requests are processed — add a layer of regulatory navigation that land-focused developers like Tract are unlikely to have in-house. As Tract assembles more sites, the cumulative power-procurement exposure across its portfolio will create demand for a coordinated, multi-site advisory relationship rather than project-by-project transactional support.

Beetaloo Energy, Halliburton
Beetaloo Energy and Halliburton signed a memorandum of understanding to co-develop up to 2 GW of gas-fired generation capacity to power a proposed data center in Weddell, Northern Territory, Australia.
Airtable  Open record
Read  A 2 GW gas-to-power data center project at the MOU stage is, by definition, a project in active partner recruitment — Halliburton's role is clearly on the drilling and completion side, leaving PPA structuring, offtake origination, and commercial power frameworks entirely unaddressed. The Northern Territory's isolated grid environment and the absence of a liquid power market make bespoke commercial structuring the central risk-management challenge for this project.
Strategic Thought  Projects of this scale in off-grid or weakly interconnected environments require the creation of synthetic market structures — long-term capacity contracts, fuel-supply hedges, and offtake agreements that replicate the price-discovery and risk-allocation functions that liquid wholesale markets would otherwise provide. The global export of U.S.-style power origination and PPA structuring expertise into markets like Australia's Northern Territory represents a nascent but growing advisory segment, as data center developers without access to established wholesale markets still require institutional-grade commercial frameworks. Beetaloo's open partner-recruitment posture signals that gas-to-power data center projects in isolated markets will increasingly seek advisors who can import structured power market architecture rather than rely on local grid convention.
