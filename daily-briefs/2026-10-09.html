1
PJM
PJM accepted its first three Expedited Interconnection Track projects — two battery storage assets and one gas uprate — totaling more than 2,100 MW, all targeting an online date of mid-2029 to serve surging data center demand.
Airtable  Open record
Read  The EIT's first acceptances validate the fast-track pathway as operational, not theoretical, and confirm that 250MW-plus capacity resources with state siting support can now move through PJM's queue on a materially compressed timeline. This sets a procedural benchmark that will inform how future DC-anchored generation deals are structured and sequenced.
Strategic Thought  For the interconnection advisory and power-origination market, EIT acceptance reorders the competitive landscape by rewarding developers who can align state siting approvals with federal queue mechanics simultaneously rather than sequentially. Originators who understand both the regulatory trigger conditions and the capacity market pricing implications of mid-2029 online dates hold a structuring advantage over generalist advisors. The two-battery-plus-gas composition of the first accepted tranche also signals that PJM is treating storage and dispatchable resources as complementary rather than competing within this track.
2
Maryland Public Service Commission  MARYLAND
Maryland's PSC awarded 15-year capacity credits to 440MW of four-hour battery storage — Flatiron Energy's 400MW Chalk Point project and REV Renewables' 40MW Jade Meadow III — falling 360MW short of the 800MW procurement target in the first round.
Airtable  Open record
Read  The shortfall against a published 800MW target in a state-administered competitive round confirms that developer supply has not yet caught up with Maryland's statutory storage mandate, creating a structurally open second round expected by January 2027. Flatiron and REV emerge from round one as credentialed participants in Maryland's capacity credit framework, with procurement track records that differentiate them heading into round two.
Strategic Thought  A 45% shortfall in a state storage procurement round is a leading indicator that interconnection queues, financing timelines, or project development capacity are constraining supply — not demand or policy intent. For PPA and capacity-credit structuring specialists, a predictable second round with a known timeline and a defined megawatt gap is among the cleaner market entry signals available in the mid-Atlantic region. The 15-year credit tenor also suggests Maryland is deliberately creating long-duration revenue certainty to de-risk project finance, which elevates the complexity and value of offtake structuring work on future submissions.
3
ThisWay Global  TEXAS
AI software company ThisWay Global acquired a bankrupt data center project at Texas A&M's RELLIS Campus in Bryan, Texas, with plans to develop a 73MW AI research and HPC facility.
Airtable  Open record
Read  An AI software company acquiring a distressed generation-adjacent asset with no apparent internal power procurement or interconnection capability represents a well-defined advisory gap at a critical early-stage decision point. The RELLIS acquisition places ThisWay Global immediately inside ERCOT's market structure, where interconnection queue positioning, load-serving arrangements, and PPA structuring decisions will be made in the near term with or without expert guidance.
Strategic Thought  Distressed asset acquisitions by non-utility technology companies are an emerging and structurally underserved entry point into the data center power market — buyers inherit site control and sometimes existing interconnection agreements but rarely inherit the energy origination expertise needed to operationalize them. In ERCOT specifically, a 73MW load obligation without a structured power supply agreement exposes an operator to nodal price volatility that can materially impair project economics before the first server rack is installed. This pattern — technology-first acquirer, energy-last strategy — is likely to repeat as more distressed data center assets surface, creating a recurring category of advisory need in markets where power market complexity is highest.

Scout Clean Energy signed a power purchase agreement with Oracle for a Texas wind energy farm, adding a major hyperscaler as an anchor offtaker in ERCOT.
Airtable  Open record
Read  A hyperscaler-anchored wind PPA in ERCOT confirms that large technology companies continue to pursue direct, long-term renewable offtake arrangements rather than relying solely on retail or broker-intermediated supply, sustaining deal flow for developers with executable projects and clean interconnection positions. Scout's ability to close with Oracle signals that its ERCOT project pipeline has reached sufficient commercial maturity to attract investment-grade counterparty commitments.
Strategic Thought  Hyperscaler-to-developer direct PPA activity in ERCOT compresses the timeline between project development and offtake execution, raising the baseline sophistication expected of wind and solar developers entering bilateral negotiations with technology company procurement teams. For the interconnection and power-origination advisory market, Scout's Oracle deal reinforces that ERCOT remains the most active bilateral PPA market in the U.S., with deal structures that require precise hedging, curtailment, and basis risk management. Advisors who can bridge generator interconnection expertise with corporate offtake structuring are positioned at the highest-value intersection of this transaction type.
